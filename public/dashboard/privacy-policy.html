Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Team 100 ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use OmniBot, its dashboard, website, API, and related services.

1. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide OmniBot's features
  • Authenticate dashboard users
  • Verify server permissions
  • Store server settings
  • Process ban appeals
  • Provide AI functionality
  • Operate giveaways
  • Operate reaction roles
  • Operate moderation features
  • Detect abuse
  • Protect the Service
  • Troubleshoot errors
  • Improve reliability
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

2. AI Processing

Some OmniBot features use third-party AI services. When AI features are used, relevant content may be sent to Groq.

This may include messages, prompts, custom personality instructions, or other information necessary to generate an AI response.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with third-party services when necessary to provide OmniBot's functionality, including Discord, Groq, and Wispbyte.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law.

4. Server Administrators

If you use OmniBot within a Discord server, the server's administrators and authorized staff may be able to access information related to the server's use of OmniBot.

For example, authorized staff may be able to access ban appeals submitted to their server.

OmniBot does not control how individual server administrators handle information they receive through Discord.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, which may include requesting access, correction, deletion, objecting to certain processing, requesting restriction of processing, or requesting a copy of certain information.

To make a privacy request, contact yoshi0490 on Discord. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

7. Children's Privacy

OmniBot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to OmniBot inappropriately, contact yoshi0490 on Discord.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

The dashboard may use cookies, local storage, session storage, or similar technologies for purposes such as authentication, maintaining sessions, saving dashboard preferences, security, and essential functionality.

9. Third-Party Links and Services

OmniBot may contain links to or integrations with third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services. You should review their privacy policies separately.

10. International Data Processing

OmniBot and its third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date will be changed when updates are made.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: yoshi0490 on Discord.
