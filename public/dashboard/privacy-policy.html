Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 21 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Team 100 handles information when you use OmniBot, the OmniBot website, dashboard, Discord bot, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Information handled through Discord

OmniBot may receive information made available through Discord when you use its features.

This can include information such as:

  • Discord user ID;
  • Discord username/display name;
  • Discord server ID;
  • Server information;
  • Messages or prompts sent to OmniBot;
  • Information necessary to provide requested features.

2. Data Storage

Team 100 does not intentionally maintain a separate permanent database of users' personal information.

OmniBot is designed to provide its functionality without intentionally maintaining permanent personal-data profiles of users.

However, information may be temporarily processed by OmniBot and/or third-party services in order to provide requested functionality.

Ban Appeals

Information submitted through a ban appeal may remain accessible to the relevant Discord server and its authorised staff.

The relevant server may retain access to appeal information according to its own configuration and practices.

3. AI Processing

When you use OmniBot's AI features, relevant prompts or content may be processed by Groq.

Groq's own data practices apply to information processed through its services.

For more information, see Groq's privacy/data policies.

4. AI Image Generation

When you request an AI-generated image, your image prompt may be sent to Pollinations AI to generate the requested image.

Pollinations AI's own privacy and data practices apply to information processed through its service.

5. Hosting

OmniBot is hosted using Wispbyte.

Technical information required to operate and secure the hosting environment may be processed by Wispbyte.

This can include information such as connection information, IP addresses, browser information, usage information, and technical logs, depending on the hosting environment.

Wispbyte's own privacy policy applies to information processed by its infrastructure.

6. Discord

Because OmniBot operates through Discord, Discord may process information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

Team 100 does not control Discord's independent processing of information.

7. Server Administrators

If you use OmniBot in a Discord server, authorised administrators and staff of that server may be able to access information related to your use of OmniBot.

This particularly applies to features such as:

  • Ban appeals;
  • Moderation;
  • Giveaways;
  • Server interactions;
  • Other server-management features.

Team 100 does not control how individual server administrators use information they receive through Discord.

8. Data Sharing

Team 100 does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to provide OmniBot, including:

  • Discord;
  • Wispbyte;
  • Groq;
  • Pollinations AI.

These providers operate independently and may process information according to their own policies.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed through OmniBot.

However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy laws may give you rights concerning your personal information.

These may include rights to:

  • Request access;
  • Request correction;
  • Request deletion;
  • Object to certain processing;
  • Request restriction of processing;
  • Request information about how your data is handled.

12. Third-Party Services

OmniBot uses third-party services including:

  • Discord — for Discord functionality, authentication, servers, users, and bot operation.
  • Wispbyte — for hosting OmniBot.
  • Groq — for AI processing.
  • Pollinations AI — for AI image generation.

Each provider has its own policies governing information processed through its services.

13. International Processing

Third-party services used by OmniBot may process information in countries other than where you live.

The privacy policies of those providers determine how their services handle such information.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Team 100 may update this Privacy Policy when OmniBot's functionality or data practices change.

The Last Updated date will be updated when changes are made.
